# PERCEPT Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

PERCEPT uses Meta's APIs to access data from authorized PERCEPT social media accounts for internal analytics and content-performance measurement.

## Requesting Data Deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your account from PERCEPT's systems, email:

wescoop23@gmail.com

Use the subject line:

**PERCEPT Data Deletion Request**

Please identify the Instagram or Facebook account associated with the request.

After receiving a valid request, PERCEPT will delete applicable stored account data and associated access credentials from its systems.

You may also revoke PERCEPT's access through your Meta or Instagram account settings.
