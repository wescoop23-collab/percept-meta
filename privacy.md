# PERCEPT Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

PERCEPT uses Meta's APIs to access information associated with authorized PERCEPT social media accounts for internal analytics and content-performance measurement.

## Information We Access

When an authorized account is connected, PERCEPT may access information made available through Meta's APIs, including:

- Instagram account profile information
- Published Instagram media
- Content performance metrics and insights
- Account-level audience and performance insights

## How We Use Information

Information accessed through Meta's APIs is used solely to analyze and understand the performance of PERCEPT's own social media accounts and content.

PERCEPT does not sell personal information or use information obtained through Meta's APIs for third-party advertising.

## Data Storage

Authorized account data may be stored securely for internal analytics and historical performance analysis. Access credentials and tokens are treated as confidential and are not intentionally made publicly available.

## Data Deletion

Requests concerning deletion of data associated with PERCEPT can be made using the instructions provided on PERCEPT's Data Deletion page.
